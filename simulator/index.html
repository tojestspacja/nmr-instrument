Checks

  • stopTX and RX on one coil The RX diodes clamp at ±0.7 V across the transmitter's ±7.95 V: 1.54 A through R813 trips the OPA564's 1.5 A limit on the first pulse. Needs a board-side decision (TX diodes + RX series element, or a second, crossed receive coil).
  • mindSignal size Exact fields, 50 mL tube: 0.90 µV at the coil, 8.8 µV at the tank, a fifth of the 40 µV the receiver was sized for (TeachSpin's estimate). ADC peak about 0.22 V, single-sample SNR about 4. Average, or use the full-bore bottle (15.9 µV).
  • mindB0 at 1.5 A The pair's real winding cross-section (28 × 27 mm) gives 2.1035 mT, not the thin-wire formula's 2.0997: the line sits at 89 560 Hz, 160 Hz above f_tx. Set f_tx to the line, or run the pair at 1.497 A.
  • ok90° pulse length All 400 turns, exactly: the second layer holds only 178 turns (x −50 … +30 mm), so B1 at the coil centre is 14.07 µT rotating and 90° takes 417 µs, as the workbook says. (The approximate model assumes an even winding and gives 451 µs.)
  • okB0 homogeneity Exact fields: 298 Hz between the most extreme voxels, but most of the water sits where the field is even: line 6 Hz FWHM, T2* 80 ms (Libbrecht's rig: about 75 ms).
  • mindB0 drift Voltage-driven, 23 W warms 9.2 kg of copper by 0.39 K/min: the line falls 138 Hz/min, 7 Hz between averaged scans against a ~2 Hz line. Drive B0 at constant current, or measure it each scan and correct f_LO.
  • okRX tank with the coax 0.78 m of RG174 adds 79 pF. Fit C710 only: tank at 88.47 kHz (−0.18 dB). All three fitted: 84.53 kHz (−3.4 dB). Or set B0 to 1.484 A.
  • okB0 drive 15.6 V of the 18 V rail at 1.5 A of the H-bridge's 2 A trip.
  • okSteel near the sample Nearest steel screw 492 mm away; the rings (r 217 mm) clear the housing.
  • okCables TX pair 590 mm · RX coax 784 mm · B0 leads 1011 mm, with 15 % slack.

Built from instrument.scad, probe.scad, housing.scad and cut-housing.scad (mechanical/ in this repository), 2 Oct 2026. The pulse experiment solves the Bloch equations for every voxel of the water (2.5 mm grid, 3555 voxels in the tube) in exact fields computed in Mathematica (build/nmr_exact.wls): Biot–Savart for the B0 pair with its real winding cross-section and for each of the class coil's 400 turns, including the direction of B1 at every voxel. With identical inputs it matches SpinDynamica (Bengs and Levitt, Magn. Reson. Chem. 2017, doi:10.1002/mrc.4642) to 0.1 % in signal, 1 Hz in line position and 1 % in T2*. Then reciprocity pickup, the Q = 10 tank and the receiver noise (15.6 nV/√Hz over 15 kHz). Shown in the frame rotating at f_tx: in the lab the magnetization turns 89 400 times a second.
